Share of pairs matched vs. pool size
01 · Transplantation

Kidney exchange: how many transplants is an optimizer worth?

Simulates kidney paired-donation pools with the Saidman generator and chooses exchange cycles with an integer program. In pools of 100 pairs the optimizer matched 55% of patients against 43% for a first-come matcher, 30% more transplants with no new donors. Blood type O patients were matched at a third of the rate of type A.

Least drug that clears infection vs. immune strength
02 · Infectious disease

Dosing antibiotics with the immune system in the loop

A PK/PD model with sensitive and resistant bacteria and a saturating innate immune response. Searching 1,000 regimens, a working immune system cut the drug needed for safe clearance 12-fold, and the requirement jumped 4.6-fold between 80% and 60% immune strength.

Methods ODE model, RK4 integration, regimen grid search, mutant selection windowRead the write-up →
Head and neck: tumor BED vs. number of fractions
03 · Radiation oncology

How many fractions? Choosing a radiotherapy schedule with the linear-quadratic model

Maximizes tumor dose at a fixed limit on late healthy-tissue damage. The optimizer recovers the direction of recent practice: about 6 large fractions for prostate, near-indifference for breast, and a 16-fraction head and neck course that finishes before tumor repopulation begins.

Methods LQ model, constrained optimization, analytical boundary checkRead the write-up →
