01 · Virology

Why most HIV exposures never become infections

Simulates the first days of HIV infection with exact Gillespie simulation and stochastic differential equations. Even with R₀ = 8, a single virion starts an infection only 14.6% of the time, and an antibody that blocks 85% of infection events cuts that risk 18-fold. Infections that do take hold reach detectable levels earlier than the deterministic model predicts.

02 · Infectious disease

Dosing antibiotics with the immune system in the loop

A PK/PD model with sensitive and resistant bacteria and a saturating innate immune response. Across 500 dosing regimens, a working immune system cut the drug needed for safe clearance 12-fold, and the requirement jumped 4.6-fold between 80% and 60% immune strength.

Methods ODE model, RK4 integration, regimen grid search, mutant selection windowRead the write-up →

03 · Radiation oncology

How many fractions? Choosing a radiotherapy schedule with the linear-quadratic model

Maximizes tumor dose at a fixed limit on late healthy-tissue damage. The optimizer recovers the direction of recent practice: about 6 large fractions for prostate, near-indifference for breast, and a 16-fraction head and neck course that finishes before tumor repopulation begins.
